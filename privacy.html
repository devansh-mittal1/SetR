Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SetR ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

SetR is designed to be highly privacy-conscious. We collect minimal information to ensure the app functions properly:

  • Local Data: All your workout templates, history, and settings are stored locally on your device using an embedded database. We do not transmit or store this data on our servers.
  • Analytics: We use an anonymous analytics provider (Mixpanel) to collect usage data (such as screen views and feature usage) to help us improve the app. This data is fully anonymized and not linked to your personal identity.
  • Subscription Data: If you purchase SetR Pro, our payment provider (RevenueCat) processes your purchase through the Apple App Store. We do not have access to your payment details (e.g., credit card information). RevenueCat only provides us with an anonymous subscriber ID to unlock your premium features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the SetR app.
  • Process your subscription unlocks via the Apple App Store.
  • Analyze anonymous usage trends to fix bugs and build better features.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • RevenueCat: Used for managing auto-renewing subscriptions. (Privacy Policy)
  • Mixpanel: Used for anonymous product analytics. (Privacy Policy)

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at studiovisionaryapps@gmail.com.
